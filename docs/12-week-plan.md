# Ci sto: 12-week plan

**30 November 2026 – 21 February 2027**

This plan shows what the team does in the next 12 weeks: from user stories and mockups to a pilot with Polimi students at the February Welcome Week. On the way, the team uses the app on its own trips first.

Max coordinates the plan and updates it each week. Dates marked *to confirm* are not decided yet. The open questions are at the end.

The slides for this plan are online: https://claude.ai/artifact/P4m8fA3gFumBNn2XBU9q2R

## Where we want to be on 21 February

1. The team used a working app prototype on at least one of its own trips.
2. The pilot at the Polimi Welcome Week has started, or everything is ready for it.
3. We know more about students and partners: survey results, interviews with venues and the first partner talks.
4. Ci sto has its first brand content from the team's trips: short videos and Instagram posts.

## The rule that shapes the plan

Nothing goes to Polimi until the team has made at least one trip with the app (kickoff agreement, 26 November 2026).

- The **Amsterdam trip (5–8 December) is the first trip with the app.** The deliverables on 2 December start from this trip. The team uses the version of the app that is ready on 5 December and writes down every problem.
- The **ski trip (8–10 January) is the second trip with the app.** It uses a better version, with the `Must` stories and the fixes from Amsterdam.
- If Amsterdam happens with the app, the rule is met. For now, the survey and the pilot stay after the ski trip. Open question 4 asks if the survey can start earlier.

Amsterdam is only three days after the deliverables, so the app for Amsterdam can only be very small. If the app is not ready for Amsterdam, the ski trip becomes the first trip with the app. If it is not ready for the ski trip either, the pilot cannot start in February. This is the biggest risk in the plan.

## Week by week

### Week 1 · 30 Nov – 6 Dec · Deliver, present and first trip with the app

- Wed 2 Dec: every person delivers their user stories and mockups, as described in the team brief. The deliverables start from the Amsterdam trip.
- Thu 3 Dec: the team presents.
- Thu 3 – Fri 4 Dec: create the Amsterdam trip in the app. Every person joins it through the app.
- Sat 5 Dec: Amsterdam starts (tentative). Use the app for the trip. Write down every problem and every idea, and what the group still did outside the app: which chats, who decided, how people paid, what went wrong. Film first content.

**Done when:** every deliverable is in the repository, the team has presented and the Amsterdam trip is in the app.

### Week 2 · 7 – 13 Dec · Merge the work

- Tue 8 Dec: Amsterdam ends. Short retro on the app: what worked, what broke and what was missing? Add the answers to the stories.
- Merge all deliverables into one version: one list of stories with final IDs and the best frames.
- Choose the stories for the prototype that the team uses on the ski trip: the `Must` stories and the fixes from Amsterdam. Without them, the team cannot plan and join a trip in the app.
- Agree who builds what, and the basic technical choices: backend, data and login.

**Done when:** there is one merged version and a short list of stories for the prototype.

### Week 3 · 14 – 20 Dec · Start building, meet partners

- App: start to build the prototype stories.
- Partners: first talks with Panto and Eterna. Both are possible partners, not confirmed.
- Validation: interview 3 or 4 bar and café owners in Bovisa and Isola about the partner model and prices.
- Check that the name is free: App Store, Instagram and domain.
- Content: publish the first Amsterdam videos.

**Done when:** the interviews are done or booked, and the name check is done.

### Week 4 · 21 – 27 Dec · Holidays

- Christmas. A light week: work only if people are available.
- App: continue to build.
- Write short notes from the interviews and partner talks.

### Week 5 · 28 Dec – 3 Jan · Holidays, test the new version

- Target: a new version of the app, with the fixes from Amsterdam, that the team can install.
- Test the app inside the team. Make a list of problems.
- Create the ski trip in the app.

**Done when:** the team can install the app and the ski trip is in it.

### Week 6 · 4 – 10 Jan · Ski trip with the app

- Mon–Thu: fix the problems that stop the trip. Every person joins the ski trip through the app.
- Fri 8 – Sun 10 Jan: ski trip. Use the app for the whole trip. Write down every problem and every idea. Film content.

**Done when:** the team has made the ski trip with the app.

### Week 7 · 11 – 17 Jan · Learn from the trip

- Retro: what worked, what broke and what was missing.
- Make one list of fixes for the pilot, with a priority for each fix.
- Content: publish the ski trip videos.
- Decide the pilot: the date of the Welcome Week and the scope (Bovisa only, or all of Polimi).
- Use the confirmed Welcome Week date to reschedule weeks 10–12. Finish the week 10 checks before the Welcome Week. Launch at the Welcome Week. Start measuring after the launch.
- If the Welcome Week starts before week 11 (8–14 Feb), bring the week 10 checks forward. Move any prep those checks depend on. Share the QR code only after the checks pass.
- If the Welcome Week starts after 21 February, end the plan with the pilot ready.
- Check the exam calendar and plan the team's time around it.

**Done when:** the pilot date and scope are decided. Weeks 10–12 follow the confirmed date. The fix list is ready.

### Week 8 · 18 – 24 Jan · Survey and fixes

- Survey Polimi students: how they meet people today, interest in networking, budget per plan and barriers.
- App: fix the most important problems from the list.
- Partners: offer a pilot deal to the venues that showed interest.
- Set the targets for the pilot (see "What we measure").
- Decide how students install the app for the pilot. Allow time for store review. If the app goes through the App Store or Google Play, submit it by the end of week 9.

**Done when:** the survey is live, the targets are set and the install method is decided.

### Week 9 · 25 – 31 Jan · Prepare the first plans

- Read the survey results. Change the pilot plan if needed.
- Prepare the plans for the first month of the pilot, with student associations and partner venues. The team report names ESN, PoliHub and venues as options; none is confirmed.
- App: continue the fixes. Build report and block.
- Submit the app for store review or set up the test version, as decided in week 8.

**Done when:** the plans for the first month are written and the survey results are summarised.

### Week 10 · Before the Welcome Week · Pilot ready

- App: freeze the pilot version. After this, only fixes.
- Check the community rules before students who do not know each other meet (team report, "Competition and risks").
- Send a test report from the frozen pilot version. Check that it reaches the team. On the same version, check that a blocked person cannot see or contact the person who blocked them.
- Put the first plans and the partner offers in the app.
- Prepare the Welcome Week material: a QR code to the app and the survey, and short videos from the trips.
- Before the first student joins, the Pilot workstream checks the data sources. Check that each app measure records test data on the frozen pilot version. Check that the attendance tally is ready and can record test attendance. Keep all test data out of the pilot numbers.
- Have a person outside the team install the app from the QR code. Confirm they installed the frozen pilot version. If a fix changes that version after any check, repeat the report, block, install and data checks. Share the QR code only after these checks pass on the version students will install.

**Done when:** the team has frozen the pilot version. The first plans are in the app. The test report, block, QR install and app data checks have passed on the version students will install. The attendance tally is ready and has recorded test attendance. The pilot counts exclude all test data.

### Week 11 · At the Welcome Week · Launch the pilot

- Launch the pilot at the Welcome Week if it starts by 21 February. If it starts later, test the launch inside the team by 21 February.
- At the launch, share the QR code.
- At the launch, help the first students and the first Organizers.

### Week 12 · Pilot and review

- Run any pilot plans scheduled from launch through 21 February. Answer students' questions quickly.
- Measure the pilot against the targets from launch through 21 February.
- Review the 12 weeks by 21 February. Use only pilot data collected by then. If the pilot has yet to launch, record that in the review. In that case, use the pre-launch checks. Write down what we learned and what changes.
- Write the next plan. Include the Dolomites trip (20–28 March) and the next intake. Cover all pilot work after 21 February, including plans and measurements.

**Done when:** the pilot has started or is ready for its date. The review is complete by 21 February. The next plan covers any pilot work after that date.

## What we measure in the pilot

We set the targets in week 8. The Pilot workstream is responsible for collecting the data. Counts start at launch. The team report lists the shared analytics events.

- Sign-ups (shared analytics events)
- "Ci sto" taps, which are requests to join a plan (shared analytics events)
- Approved requests (shared analytics events)
- Confirmed places (shared analytics events)
- People who came to the plan (attendance tally). The Organizer or the team counts who came at each plan.
- New Cerchia connections (shared analytics events)

## Risks

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| The app is not ready for Amsterdam, three days after the deliverables. | Keep the Amsterdam version very small. Do the rest in the group chats and write down what the app was missing. The ski trip then becomes the first trip with the app. |
| The app is not ready for the ski trip, so the pilot cannot start in February. | Keep the prototype small: only the `Must` stories. Check progress at the end of weeks 3 and 5. |
| Christmas, New Year and exams leave less time. | Plan light work in weeks 4 and 5. Check the exam calendar in week 7. |
| No partner is confirmed. | Start talks in week 3. The team can seed the first plans itself, as the team report proposes. |
| Students cannot install the app in time. | Decide the install method in week 8. Submit the app for store review or set up the test version in week 9. In week 10, have a person outside the team install the app from the QR code. Share the code only after the week 10 checks pass on the version students will install. |
| Amsterdam does not happen. | The ski trip becomes the first trip with the app. |
