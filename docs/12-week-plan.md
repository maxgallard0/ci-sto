# Ci sto: 12-week plan

**30 November 2026 – 21 February 2027**

This plan shows what the team does in the next 12 weeks: from user stories and mockups to a pilot with Polimi students at the February Welcome Week. On the way, the team uses the app on its own trips first.

Max coordinates the plan and updates it each week. Dates marked *to confirm* are not decided yet. The open questions are at the end.

## Where we want to be on 21 February

1. The team used a working app prototype on at least one of its own trips.
2. The pilot at the Polimi Welcome Week has started, or everything is ready for it.
3. We know more about students and partners: survey results, interviews with venues and the first partner talks.
4. Ci sto has its first brand content from the team's trips: short videos and Instagram posts.

## The rule that shapes the plan

Nothing goes to Polimi until the team has made at least one trip with the app (kickoff agreement, 26 November 2026).

- Amsterdam is only three days after the mockups are delivered, so the app cannot be ready for it. The team uses Amsterdam to see how it organises a trip today and to make first content.
- The **ski trip (8–10 January) is the first trip with the app.** The app must be ready for the team to plan and join that trip.
- Everything that goes to Polimi students comes after the ski trip: the survey and the pilot.

If the app is not ready for the ski trip, the pilot cannot start in February. This is the biggest risk in the plan.

## Week by week

### Week 1 · 30 Nov – 6 Dec · Deliver and present

- Wed 2 Dec: every person delivers their user stories and mockups, as described in the team brief.
- Thu 3 Dec: the team presents.
- Sat 5 Dec: Amsterdam starts (tentative). Write down how the group organises the trip today: which chats, who decides, how people pay, what goes wrong. Film first content.

**Done when:** every deliverable is in the repository and the team has presented.

### Week 2 · 7 – 13 Dec · Merge the work

- Tue 8 Dec: Amsterdam ends. Short retro: what was hard to organise? Add the answers to the stories.
- Merge all deliverables into one version: one list of stories with final IDs and the best frames.
- Choose the stories for the prototype: the `Must` stories, without which the team cannot plan and join the ski trip in the app.
- Agree who builds what, and the basic technical choices: backend, data and login.

**Done when:** there is one merged version and a short list of stories for the prototype.

### Week 3 · 14 – 20 Dec · Start building, meet partners

- App: start to build the prototype stories.
- Partners: first talks with Panto and Eterna. Both are possible partners, not confirmed.
- Validation: interview 3 or 4 bar and café owners in Bovisa and Isola about the partner model and prices.
- Check that the name is free: App Store, Instagram and domain.
- Content: publish the first Amsterdam videos.

**Done when:** the interviews are done or booked, and the name check is done.

### Week 4 · 21 – 27 Dec · Holidays

- Christmas. A light week: work only if people are available.
- App: continue to build.
- Write short notes from the interviews and partner talks.

### Week 5 · 28 Dec – 3 Jan · Holidays, first test of the app

- Target: a first version of the app that the team can install.
- Test the app inside the team. Make a list of problems.
- Create the ski trip in the app.

**Done when:** the team can install the app and the ski trip is in it.

### Week 6 · 4 – 10 Jan · First trip with the app

- Mon–Thu: fix the problems that stop the trip. Every person joins the ski trip through the app.
- Fri 8 – Sun 10 Jan: ski trip. Use the app for the whole trip. Write down every problem and every idea. Film content.

**Done when:** the team has made one trip with the app. From now on, the team can go to Polimi.

### Week 7 · 11 – 17 Jan · Learn from the trip

- Retro: what worked, what broke and what was missing.
- Make one list of fixes for the pilot, with a priority for each fix.
- Content: publish the ski trip videos.
- Decide the pilot: the date of the Welcome Week and the scope (Bovisa only, or all of Polimi).
- Check the exam calendar and plan the team's time around it.

**Done when:** the pilot date and scope are decided and the fix list is ready.

### Week 8 · 18 – 24 Jan · Survey and fixes

- Survey Polimi students: how they meet people today, interest in networking, budget per plan and barriers.
- App: fix the most important problems from the list.
- Partners: offer a pilot deal to the venues that showed interest.
- Set the targets for the pilot (see "What we measure").
- Decide how students install the app for the pilot. Allow time for store review. If the app goes through the App Store or Google Play, submit it by the end of week 9.

**Done when:** the survey is live, the targets are set and the install method is decided.

### Week 9 · 25 – 31 Jan · Prepare the first plans

- Read the survey results. Change the pilot plan if needed.
- Prepare the plans for the first month of the pilot, with student associations and partner venues. The team report names ESN, PoliHub and venues as options; none is confirmed.
- App: continue the fixes. Build report and block.
- Submit the app for store review or set up the test version, as decided in week 8.

**Done when:** the plans for the first month are written and the survey results are summarised.

### Week 10 · 1 – 7 Feb · Pilot ready

- App: freeze the pilot version. After this, only fixes.
- Check the community rules before students who do not know each other meet (team report, "Competition and risks").
- Send a test report from the frozen pilot version. Check that it reaches the team. On the same version, check that a blocked person cannot see or contact the person who blocked them.
- Put the first plans and the partner offers in the app.
- Prepare the Welcome Week material: a QR code to the app and the survey, and short videos from the trips.
- Have a person outside the team install the app from the QR code. Confirm they installed the frozen pilot version. If a fix changes that version after any check, repeat the report, block and install checks. Share the QR code only after these checks pass on the version students will install.

**Done when:** the team has frozen the pilot version. The first plans are in the app. The test report, block and QR install checks have passed on the version students will install from the QR code.

### Week 11 · 8 – 14 Feb · Welcome Week launch

- Launch the pilot at the Welcome Week, if it takes place this week or the next. Otherwise, use this week to test the launch inside the team.
- Be at the Welcome Week with the QR code.
- Help the first students and the first Organizers.

### Week 12 · 15 – 21 Feb · Pilot and review

- Run the first plans. Answer students' questions quickly.
- Measure the pilot against the targets.
- Review the 12 weeks: what we learned and what changes.
- Write the next plan: the Dolomites trip (20–28 March) and the next intake.

**Done when:** the pilot has started (or is ready for its date) and the next plan exists.

## What we measure in the pilot

We set the numbers in week 8. The measures come from the shared analytics events in the team report:

- Sign-ups
- "Ci sto" taps (requests to join a plan)
- Approved requests
- Confirmed places
- People who came to the plan
- New Cerchia connections

## Risks

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| The app is not ready for the ski trip, so the pilot cannot start in February. | Keep the prototype small: only the `Must` stories. Check progress at the end of weeks 3 and 5. |
| Christmas, New Year and exams leave less time. | Plan light work in weeks 4 and 5. Check the exam calendar in week 7. |
| No partner is confirmed. | Start talks in week 3. The team can seed the first plans itself, as the team report proposes. |
| Students cannot install the app in time. | Decide the install method in week 8. Submit the app for store review or set up the test version in week 9. In week 10, have a person outside the team install the app from the QR code. Share the code only after the week 10 checks pass on the version students will install. |
| Amsterdam does not happen. | Nothing in the plan depends on it. Use another moment to observe how the team organises a trip. |
