# Team messages

Copies of the messages that Max sends to the team group chat on WhatsApp. To use one, copy the text and paste it into the chat. The `*bold*` marks are WhatsApp formatting.

If a message and [deliverables/README.md](../deliverables/README.md) say different things, follow the README and update the message.

## Welcome

For people who join the group. Send it together with the zip of the team guides.

```text
Welcome to Ci sto!

Ci sto (Italian for "I'm in") is an app to find plans and people: trips and events, with recommendations from people you trust. Together we design the mobile app, starting from our own trips. You don't need work experience, design software or coding skills.

*The zip*
The zip in this chat has everything for your first deliverable. The same files are on GitHub: open the repository, then Code → Download ZIP.

What is inside:
- deliverables/README.md: start here. What you deliver, the steps, the dates and how to upload your work. It takes about 15 minutes to read.
- deliverables/learn.md: short lessons for beginners on problem definition, user stories, acceptance criteria and mockups, with examples and exercises.
- deliverables/_template.md: the template for your deliverable file.
- skills/: step-by-step instructions for your AI, one folder per task: problem definition, user stories, mockup briefs and your deliverable file. The synthesis skill is for later, when we merge everyone's work.
- docs/design-system.md: the colours, type and components for the app mockups.
- docs/12-week-plan.md: our plan from 30 Nov to 21 Feb, ending with the pilot at the February Welcome Week.
- AGENTS.md: the project rules for any AI assistant. You can read it too.
- You can ignore CLAUDE.md and the deliverables/synthesis folder.

*How to start*
1. Unzip it and read deliverables/README.md from top to bottom.
2. Get GitHub access: make an account if you don't have one, send me your GitHub username, then accept the invitation email from GitHub.
3. On day one, create your branch with your name (team/<yourname>, for example team/elena) and open your pull request (README section 10).
4. Ask the AI you use to read deliverables/learn.md and explain it to you in more detail.
5. Think on your own for 20 minutes with the questions in README section 8, before any AI.
6. Open a new chat with your AI, attach the 9 files listed in README section 9, and send the coach prompt from that section. Your AI guides you step by step. It helps you think, but it does not do the work for you.

Base your deliverable on our Amsterdam trip: the first trip that we plan and make with the app.

*Dates*
- Wed 2 Dec: your pull request is complete
- Thu 3 Dec: we present together
- 5–8 Dec: Amsterdam, our first trip with the app (tentative)

Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/P4m8fA3gFumBNn2XBU9q2R

Questions about the brief: ask me. Questions about GitHub: ask here in the group.
```

## First deliverable

The files and the steps for the first deliverable. Send it together with the zip of the team guides.

```text
Hi everyone! Here are the files for our first Ci sto deliverable (zip attached). The same files are on GitHub: open the repository, then Code → Download ZIP.

*Focus: our Amsterdam trip*
Base your deliverable on our Amsterdam trip (5–8 Dec). It is the first trip that we plan and make with the app. Imagine it in Ci sto: the Students who join it, the Organizer who plans it and the Partners it could use, for example a hostel or a venue.

*New: define the problem first*
Before you write stories, you now define the problem for each user (Student, Organizer, Partner): context, profile, job, 3 to 5 frictions, point of view and "How might we". Mark each friction Observed (you lived it or saw it) or Assumption (you think it is true). Then link each story to a friction of the same user.

*What to do*
1. Unzip it and start with deliverables/README.md. It explains everything.
2. Get GitHub access: make an account if you don't have one, send me your GitHub username, then accept the invitation email from GitHub.
3. On day one, create your branch with your name (team/<yourname>, for example team/elena) and open your pull request (README section 10, steps A, B and D). Don't wait until your work is finished.
4. Ask the AI you use to read deliverables/learn.md and explain it to you in more detail.
5. Think on your own for 20 minutes with the questions in README section 8, before any AI.
6. Open a new chat with your AI, attach the 9 files listed in README section 9, and send the coach prompt from that section. Your AI will guide you step by step.
7. Define the problem for our three users, then write your stories and draw at least one mockup frame for each story.
8. Commit your progress to your branch after each step, until Wednesday. Your open pull request shows me how you are doing (README section 10, step E).

*Dates*
- Wed 2 Dec: your pull request is complete
- Thu 3 Dec: we present together
- 5–8 Dec: Amsterdam, our first trip with the app (tentative)

The 12-week plan (30 Nov – 21 Feb, ending with the pilot at the February Welcome Week) is in docs/12-week-plan.md.

Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/P4m8fA3gFumBNn2XBU9q2R

Questions about the brief: ask me. Questions about GitHub: ask here in the group.
```

## Commitment

Asks each person if they want to be part of the project. The expectations are also in [team-agreements.md](team-agreements.md).

```text
Hi everyone,

Before we go further with Ci sto, I want to ask each of you one honest question: do you really want to be part of this project?

It is fine to say no. I prefer a clear answer now to a half yes. If you are in, this is what I expect from each person:

- Progress every week, not only before a deadline.
- Teamwork: share your work, give feedback and help each other.
- Commitment: do what you say you will do, and tell the group early if you can't.
- A weekly meeting with the whole team. We will agree on the day and time together.

The first step is this Wednesday, 2 December:
- Your own branch with your name: team/<yourname>
- Your commits on it, with your progress
- An open pull request with your work so far

Please reply here or in private as soon as you can: "Ci sto" if you are in, or "not this time" if you are out. No hard feelings either way.
```
