# Team agreements

How we work together on Ci sto. Max coordinates the work. The agreements from the kickoff on 26 November 2026 are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#agreements-from-the-kickoff-26-november-2026).

## What Max expects from each person

- **Progress every week,** not only before a deadline.
- **Teamwork:** share your work, give feedback and help each other.
- **Commitment:** do what you say you will do, and tell the group early if you can't.
- **A weekly meeting** with the whole team. We agree on the day and time together. Day and time: to confirm.

## Are you in?

Max asks each person for a clear answer. Reply in the team group chat or in private: "Ci sto" if you are in, or "not this time" if you are out. Both answers are fine.

## Your work in the repository

- Work on your own branch, with your name: `team/<name>`.
- Open your pull request on the first day. Commit your progress after each step. Your open pull request shows Max how you are doing.
- Your first deadline is Wednesday 2 December 2026. By then, your pull request has your complete deliverable.

The steps are in [deliverables/README.md](../deliverables/README.md), section 10. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#rules-for-the-repository).

## Team group chat

Max sends the main messages to the team group chat. Copies are in [team-messages.md](team-messages.md).
